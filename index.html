The preceding brief history of global earth science gives a wonderfully incomplete record of the subject. Many important contributions and contributors are omitted. But it should provide some background on the general flow of the subject for those uninitiated in earth science and, hence, provide a setting for some of the examples in the rest of the book.

The cat, a tabby, sat. The dog—a puppy—ran. The bird (a robin) flew. The fish swam; the pond was calm! The frog jumped: splash. The horse trotted, then stopped. The cow grazed (slowly). The pig oinked: "oink, oink"? The duck quacked, 'quack-quack'. The hen clucked; the chicks' heads turned. The sheep bleated—loudly. The goat climbed (the fence).
