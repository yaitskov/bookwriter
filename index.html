Adaptive E-reader C2

Weclome to C2 bookreader! It's a bookreader dedicated for ESL readers that adapts a book content to user's language level. If a book vocabulary is too hard for you, lower down the threshold for the minimum allowed word frequency in the app settings.

There is no an open book. Click or press o key to choose a book from the device.

Click or press h key to see keyboard shortcuts and mouse actions.
